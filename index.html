Quick formula

Article + noun

el libro = the book

la mesa = the table

Simple explanation

In Spanish, many nouns have a gender. el is used for masculine nouns, and la is used for feminine nouns.

Formula

Use this pattern: article + noun
Example: el perro = the dog

Worked example

la mochila = the backpack
Here, la matches the feminine noun mochila.

Common mistakes

  • Using el instead of la for feminine words.
  • Forgetting that Spanish nouns can be masculine or feminine.
  • Mixing up the word meaning when memorising vocabulary.
